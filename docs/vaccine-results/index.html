What strict and loose actually allow

These are curated tissue-expression definitions. They do not establish that a tissue is immune privileged or that targeting it is safe.

Strict · core reproductive tissues

Testis, ovary and placenta. These tissues enter the core reproductive RNA fraction.

Loose · extended reproductive tract

The strict tissues plus cervix, endometrium, epididymis, fallopian tube, prostate, seminal vesicle and vagina.

Neither RNA numerator includes breast or lactating breast. OncoRef applies its curated reproductive-fraction and protein-expression rules, including exceptions; these are not absolute zero-expression gates. Thymus remains in the default RNA fraction denominator but is excluded from the somatic maximum and reproductive protein flag. Protein restriction conventions also use broader reproductive tissue labels. Each definition changes which protein isoforms count as non-CTA background.

Which cancers drive the priority?

A protein scores higher when it is highly expressed in more samples from cancers that account for more deaths worldwide.

What “p95 occurrence” means

The percentage of cancer samples in which a protein ranks in the top 5% of expression within that sample.

For example, 30% means the protein reaches that expression rank in 30 out of 100 measured samples. It does not mean the vaccine covers 30% of patients.

The ranking adds these contributions across cancer categories. Incidence is shown for context and is not multiplied into the score.

The inclusion rule

A longer observed peptide does not support an unobserved shorter sequence. HLA reach uses measured and inferred assignments as explicitly labeled; MS observations do not validate predicted HLA restrictions or establish T-cell recognition.

  3. Require observed ligand evidence
  4. Assemble within the length limits

      
How to interpret this design
This is a research antigen design. Expression ranking, normal-tissue restriction, observed peptides and binding predictions do not establish clinical efficacy or safety. Remaining junctional predictions need review and experimental validation.
Mortality and incidence values are shares of reference totals. Absolute counts are not available for every category. Cancer sample overlap and clinical patient coverage are not calculated.

      
Where the data come from
Carrier calculations follow the locus-aware assumptions described by Bui and colleagues. The worldwide donor-registry frequency reference is CIWD 3.0, as tabulated in Table A2 of Lőrincz and colleagues. These donors are a frequency proxy, not a census-weighted worldwide population.
